# My Hero Academia
A brief overview of my favorite anime series.

---

## 📌 Quick Summary
**My Hero Academia** is a superhero anime series created by **Kohei Horikoshi**. Set in a world where 80% of the population possesses superpowers called **"Quirks,"** the story follows **Izuku Midoriya**, a Quirkless boy who dreams of becoming the world's greatest hero.

---

## ⭐ Key Information

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Genre** | Superhero, Action, Coming-of-Age |
| **Studio** | BONES |
| **Main Character** | Izuku Midoriya (Deku) |
| **Number 1 Hero** | All Might |

---

## 🏆 Top Reasons to Watch
1. **Inspiring Character Growth** – Deku's journey from powerlessness to heroics is deeply motivating.
2. **Diverse Cast of Heroes & Villains** – Unique designs, personalities, and Quirk abilities.
3. **Epic Battles & Soundtrack** – High-stakes fights backed by hype music like *You Say Run*.
